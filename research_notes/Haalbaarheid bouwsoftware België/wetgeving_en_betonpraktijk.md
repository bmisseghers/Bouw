## 1. Mandatory structured B2B e-invoicing via Peppol (since 1 Jan 2026), e-reporting 2028, how a vendor connects

### Takeaway
B2B structured e-invoicing is in force since 1 Jan 2026 with Peppol BIS/UBL as default; the three-month penalty tolerance ended 31 March 2026 and fines (EUR 1,500 / 3,000 / 5,000) can now be imposed. For a niche construction package the realistic route is an API of an existing Peppol provider (roughly EUR 0.10-0.30 per document) rather than becoming an Access Point; near-real-time e-reporting is planned for 1 Jan 2028 but the law was still only a preliminary draft in mid-2026.

### Cited Findings
- The B2B e-invoicing obligation applies since 1 January 2026; the general tolerance period and the special tolerance for technically unsupported self-billing have both expired — [Unizo](https://www.unizo.be/ondernemersgids/financieel/factuur/e-facturatie-correct-werken-met-peppol-en-je-digitale)
- The tolerance only postponed administrative fines until end of March 2026 for businesses that could show they had taken timely steps; the obligation itself applied from 1 Jan 2026 — [Banqup](https://www.banqup.com/nl-be/resources/blog/e-facturatie-tolerantieperiode-2026-belgie)
- The tolerance ended 31 March 2026 and was not extended — [lookuptax](https://lookuptax.com/docs/einvoicing/peppol-einvoicing-belgium)
- Fines: EUR 1,500 first infringement, EUR 3,000 second, EUR 5,000 from the third — [Practicali](https://www.practicali.be/blog/e-facturering-tolerantieperiode-eerste-drie-maanden-2026); same scale in [Edicom](https://edicomgroup.com/nl/blog/belgie-gaat-b2b-elektronische-facturatie-verplicht-stellen)
- The tax authority does not have to issue a warning before fining — [Wolters Kluwer](https://www.wolterskluwer.com/nl-be/expert-insights/e-invoicing-2026-fines-up-to-5000)
- Default format is Peppol BIS in UBL; another format/transmission channel is allowed only if both parties agree (it must still be a structured EN 16931 invoice per the secondary sources) — [lookuptax](https://lookuptax.com/docs/einvoicing/peppol-einvoicing-belgium)
- Exclusions reported: suppliers doing only VAT-exempt activities under art. 44 WBTW; some exempt customers do not have to receive; one source says flat-rate (forfaitaire) VAT payers are exempt until 1 Jan 2028 (not confirmed officially) — [Accountable help](https://help.accountable.eu/en/articles/12629021-peppol-for-franchisees-and-vat-exempt-businesses-what-you-need-to-know), [lookuptax](https://lookuptax.com/docs/einvoicing/peppol-einvoicing-belgium)
- E-reporting: near-real-time reporting planned for January 2028 as complement to e-invoicing — [Edicom](https://edicomgroup.com/nl/blog/belgie-gaat-b2b-elektronische-facturatie-verplicht-stellen), [Comarch](https://www.comarch.com/trade-and-services/data-management/legal-regulation-changes/belgium-confirms-2026-peppol-e-invoicing-mandate-with-2028-near-real-time-reporting-to-follow/)
- Council of Ministers approved a preliminary draft law (voorontwerp) on 18 July 2026; the 1 Jan 2028 start is subject to adoption; the system is a five-corner model inside Peppol — [Banqup compliance pulse](https://www.banqup.com/nl-be/resources/compliance-pulse/belgie)
- Provider pricing (indicative, 2026):
  - Recommand: free 25 docs/month then EUR 0.30/doc; Starter EUR 29/month incl. 200 docs then EUR 0.20; Professional EUR 99/month incl. 1,000 docs then EUR 0.10; open-source API — [openbankingtracker](https://openbankingtracker.com/peppol-providers)
  - e-invoice.be: Pro EUR 0.25/invoice (packages from EUR 350), Enterprise EUR 0.18/invoice with volume discounts (stated current May 2026) — [openbankingtracker](https://openbankingtracker.com/peppol-providers)
  - Billit: sources disagree — "paid licences from EUR 7.50/month, free Peppol sending" ([e-invoice.app](https://www.e-invoice.app/vendors/billit)) versus "EUR 7.50/month for first 25 invoices then EUR 0.30/invoice" in a partner integration ([Rentman](https://rentman.io/integrations/billit)); official page: [Billit pricing](https://www.billit.eu/pricing)
  - Digiteal (integrator plan): one-off setup EUR 365 excl. VAT, pay-per-document, monthly minimum EUR 85 excl. VAT prepaid — [Digiteal](https://www.digiteal.eu/pricing/)
  - Storecove: volume-based transaction pricing, free 30-day sandbox, no public per-document rate — [e-invoice.app](https://www.e-invoice.app/vendors/storecove)
  - Odoo advertises free unlimited Peppol send/receive inside its own product — [Odoo](https://www.odoo.com/electronic-invoicing/belgium)
- Becoming an Access Point yourself: requires OpenPeppol membership (signed form, statutes, subscription fees), then a Peppol Service Provider Agreement with a Peppol Authority; fee structure = sign-up fee + annual fee + AP certification fee; for organisations >2,500 employees the page shows sign-up 2,950 and annual 5,500 (smaller tiers exist; check table) — [OpenPeppol fees](https://peppol.org/join/fees/), [OpenPeppol AP how-to](https://peppol.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/how_to_set-up_a__post-award__peppol_access_point_v2.1.pdf)
- OpenPeppol has made ISO certification mandatory for all Access Points (exact standard not confirmed in extract) — [Storecove blog](https://www.storecove.com/blog/en/peppol-standards-iso-certification)

### Inferences
- Software must: (a) produce EN 16931-compliant UBL (Peppol BIS Billing 3.0) invoices AND credit notes from every outgoing B2B invoice, including vorderingsstaat invoices; (b) look up the customer's Peppol participant ID (KBO/VAT number based) before sending; (c) receive incoming supplier invoices via Peppol and feed them into aankopen/nacalculatie (line-level matching to bestelbon/leveringsbon is a strong feature because the UBL is structured); (d) store send/receive status and the original XML; (e) keep a PDF path for B2C customers (particulieren), who are outside the mandate.
- Construction-specific UBL content to map: tax category "AE" (reverse charge / btw verlegd) with exemption reason text; 6% lines with the statutory declaration as invoice note; retention (inhouding 30bis) is a payment-side matter and should NOT reduce the invoice total; price revision and waarborg lines as allowance/charge or separate lines; attachment of the vorderingsstaat PDF as embedded document.
- Build vs buy: AP status means membership fees, ISO certification, AS4 infrastructure and an authority agreement — disproportionate for a start-up. A provider API costs about EUR 0.10-0.30 per document, i.e. for a contractor sending/receiving 300 docs/month about EUR 30-100/month, which can be absorbed in the subscription or passed through.
- Design the invoice data model now so a 2028 e-reporting feed (five-corner, provider reports to the tax authority) is handled by the provider without rework; choose a provider that states it will support it.

### Gaps
- Legal basis not opened: believed to be the wet van 6 februari 2024 (amending WBTW) plus a KB of July 2025 on technical modalities — background knowledge, unverified in this session.
- Not verified: the increased investment deduction / cost deduction for e-invoicing software, the "round VAT per invoice total" rule from 2026, the list of named providers Banqup/Unifiedpost, CodaBox, Ixor, Dokapi, Babelway, Tickstar pricing (no public prices found; Dokapi described as pay-as-you-go unpublished).
- ViDA dates (EU cross-border e-invoicing/digital reporting, widely reported as 1 July 2030) not sourced here.
- BOSA as Belgian Peppol Authority and its onboarding conditions not confirmed by a source in this session.
- No reliable source found on what enforcement actually looked like after April 2026 (number of fines).

## 2. Vorderingsstaten, meetstaat, min-/meerwerken, prijsherziening, borgstelling

### Takeaway
In public works the payment cycle is a statutory 30 days verification + 30 days payment (60 total) triggered by the contractor's schuldvordering with a detailed vorderingsstaat; price revision is by formula with wage and material indices (old I-index, I-2021 since Jan 2021, Flemish catalogue of standard formulas); the 5% borgtocht is released half at provisional and half at final acceptance. Private works follow the contract, capped by the late-payment law (max 60 days, verification included).

### Cited Findings
- Public contracts concluded after 16 March 2013: payment term split into a verification period and a payment period of 30 days each (60 in total) — [Belfius newsletter](https://www.belfius.be/common/NL/multimedia/MMDownloadableFile/Newsletters/BelfiusContact/MMDF%20Contact%208%20Markets.pdf); see also [Lexgo](https://www.lexgo.be/nl/nieuws-en-publicaties/1806-algemene-uitvoeringsregels-voor-de-overheidsopdrachten-betalingstermijnen-verwijlinteresten)
- The KB Uitvoering (14 Jan 2013) payment rules apply in principle to contracts with estimated value above EUR 8,500 (threshold as stated in the source; later amended — verify) — [Lexgo](https://www.lexgo.be/nl/nieuws-en-publicaties/1806-algemene-uitvoeringsregels-voor-de-overheidsopdrachten-betalingstermijnen-verwijlinteresten)
- A repair-KB of 22 May 2014 aligned the payment rules with Directive 2011/7/EU and clarified the verification period — [Vlaanderen, Bestuurszaken](https://assets.vlaanderen.be/image/upload/v1668429487/Document_van_bestuurszaken_wijzigingen_reparatie-KB_van_22_mei_2014_in_KB_b1llpm.pdf)
- Terminology: the "vorderingsstaat" is strictly the authority's document listing, per period, the performances accepted for payment; the contractor files a "schuldvordering"; practice mixes the terms. Late payment gives interest plus a EUR 40 flat recovery fee (art. 69 KB) — [MOW dienstorder verwijlintresten](https://dienstverlening-mow.vlaanderen.be/sites/default/files/2018-08/Dienstorder%20verwijlintresten_0.pdf)
- VMSW shows monthly price-revision calculation per vorderingsstaat with a coefficient per period (VM/B 2013 rubric prijsherziening) — [VMSW example vorderingsstaat](https://assets.vlaanderen.be/image/upload/v1669212489/Vorderingsstaat_voor_bereik_limiet_oygpuk.pdf)
- The old I-index (bouwmaterialen-index, in use since 1955) is succeeded by the I-2021 index: usable for new contracts from January 2021, or existing ones by mutual agreement; both co-exist; I-2021 has 60 references in 11 product groups and no longer includes petroleum products — [Lydian via legalnews](https://legalnews.be/bouw-vastgoed/vervanging-van-de-bouwmaterialen-index-door-een-nieuwe-i-2021-index-lydian/)
- I-2021 does not automatically apply to public contracts already in execution; the old index stays where the formula refers to it — [GDA via legalnews](https://legalnews.be/overheidsopdrachten/overheidsopdrachten-de-overstap-naar-de-i-2021-index-vangt-aan-wat-houdt-dit-in-voor-uw-lopende-nieuwe-overheidsopdracht-gda-advocaten/)
- Flemish MOW publishes a catalogue of standardised price-revision formulas (v2.0, July 2025) of the form p = P x (sum of weights x current/base index), using wage indices and NACE-based material indices (base 2021=100) — [MOW catalogus prijsherzieningsformules 2.0](https://dienstverlening-mow.vlaanderen.be/system/files/2025-07/20251707_DV_catalogus_prijsherzieningsformules%20%282.0%29.pdf)
- Public works: after provisional acceptance the contractor can request payment of the balance and release of half of the borgtocht; second half at final acceptance (one year later for social housing) — [Vlaanderen, oplevering](https://www.vlaanderen.be/lokaal-bestuur/overheidsopdrachten/oplevering), [Vlaanderen sociaal woonbeleid](https://www.vlaanderen.be/sociaal-woonbeleid/sociale-woningen/realiseren-of-renoveren-van-sociale-woonprojecten/woningbouw-en-infrastructuur/oplevering/oplevering-van-dossiers-met-enkel-bouw-met-enkel-gesubsidieerde-infrastructuuraanleg-of-van-gecombineerde-dossiers)
- Borgtocht in public contracts is commonly 5% of the original contract amount excl. VAT; a reform discussed by Publius eases the rules (art. 25 exceptions; release on the authority's own initiative, art. 33) — [Publius via legalnews](https://legalnews.be/overheidsopdrachten/over-de-borgtocht-in-overheidsopdrachten-publius/)
- The borgtocht can be adjusted when modifications exceed 20% of the contract amount (source cites art. 28) — [Vlaanderen sociaal woonbeleid, uitvoering](https://www.vlaanderen.be/sociaal-woonbeleid/sociale-woningen/realiseren-of-renoveren-van-sociale-woonprojecten/woningbouw-en-infrastructuur/uitvoering/dossiers-met-bouw-gesubsidieerde-infrastructuur-of-gecombineerde-dossiers)
- Private B2B: since 1 Feb 2022 payment terms may contractually be extended to max 60 days for all companies; a verification period counts inside that term; contrary clauses are deemed unwritten; EUR 40 flat fee due by law — [Credendo](https://credendo.com/nl/knowledge-hub/aangepaste-betalingstermijnen-vanaf-1-februari-2022), [Odigo via legalnews](https://legalnews.be/handel-consument/de-wijzigingen-aan-de-wet-betalingsachterstand-vanaf-1-februari-2022-een-kort-overzicht-odigo-advocaten/)

### Inferences
- Core data model: contract meetstaat (posts with code, description, unit, quantity type VH = vermoedelijke hoeveelheid / FH = forfaitaire hoeveelheid, unit price) -> per period a vorderingsstaat with per post: previous cumulative quantity, quantity this period, new cumulative, % executed, amount; totals cumulative minus previously certified = amount this period. Separate sections for meerwerken/minwerken (verrekeningen) with their own approval status and numbering, and for price revision.
- Calculations to automate: (1) cumulative/previous/current logic; (2) price revision per period with configurable formula (weights a, b, c; base indices at tender date; current indices per execution month) and an index table that is updated monthly (S wage index per category, I, I-2021, MOW/NACE indices) — formula must be configurable per project because public bodies each prescribe their own; (3) retention/waarborg (private: contractual % withheld per invoice and released at oplevering; public: borgtocht is a separate guarantee, not a deduction); (4) VAT regime per project; (5) due-date clock: date schuldvordering filed, verification deadline (+30), payment deadline (+30), automatic interest + EUR 40 calculation.
- Workflow states: draft -> sent to architect/leidend ambtenaar -> approved (possibly with corrected quantities) -> invoice generated only after approval (public sector: invoice follows the approved amount). The software must allow the approved quantities to differ from claimed ones and carry the difference forward.
- Output: Excel/PDF in the layout the client expects (VMSW, AWV, architect templates differ) plus the Peppol invoice referencing the vorderingsstaat number.

### Gaps
- The exact text of KB 14 Jan 2013 (art. 66, 95, 25-33, 38/1-38/19 on modifications incl. the 2017+ review clauses) was not read; article numbers above are from secondary sources.
- The classic formula p = P (a s/S + b i/I + c) and typical weights (e.g. 0.40/0.40/0.20) and ABEX-index use in private contracts and insurance: no source found in this session (background knowledge, unverified).
- Typical private-works waarborg-inhouding practice (5% or 10%, bank guarantee replacement) not sourced.
- Whether the borgtocht reform described by Publius is in force and from when: not established.

## 3. Inhoudingsplicht and hoofdelijke aansprakelijkheid (art. 30bis RSZ-wet, art. 403-404 WIB92) — incl. the new 2026 RSVZ withholding

### Takeaway
Before every payment to a (sub)contractor for works in immovable state the payer must check debts and, if there are debts, withhold 35% (RSZ/sector fund) and/or 15% (tax); new in 2026 is a 15% withholding for debts of the self-employed contributions (RSVZ), with a combined cap of 50%. Checks can be done via checkinhoudingsplicht.be or a secured webservice (BillRetainment); this must be automated at payment time.

### Cited Findings
- Withholding percentages: 35% for RSZ and/or the sector fund (PDOK/FBZB), 15% for RSVZ; fiscal debts 15%; combined ceiling 50% when several debts coincide — [socialsecurity.be FAQ inhouding op factuur](https://www.socialsecurity.be/site_nl/employer/applics/30bis/general/invoice.htm)
- Consultation channels: the secured webservice "BillRetainment" and the website checkinhoudingsplicht.be — [socialsecurity.be FAQ](https://www.socialsecurity.be/site_nl/employer/applics/30bis/general/invoice.htm), portal: [socialsecurity.be 30bis](https://www.socialsecurity.be/site_nl/employer/applics/30bis/index.htm)
- Conflict on webservice scope: one source says a new "Billretainment" webservice launched in May 2026 bundles RSZ, RSVZ and FOD Financiën results in one answer ([Forum for the Future](https://blog.forumforthefuture.be/nl/article/nieuwe-inhoudingsplicht-vanaf-1-mei-2026/31059)); Securex says the webservice currently only covers RSZ social debts ([Securex](https://www.securex.be/nl/lex4you/zelfstandige/rechten-plichten/hoofdberoep/plichten/inschrijving-in-de-kbo/de-inhoudingsplicht-in-de-bouw-en-schoonmaaksector-vanaf-1-01-2026)); Partena says it will be extended to the self-employed status sector ([Partena](https://www.partena-professional.be/nl/onze-inzichten/infoflashes/inhoudingsplicht-de-bouw-en-schoonmaaksector))
- Conflict on start date of the RSVZ withholding: legal entry 1 Jan 2026 (Securex title), "new withholding requirement as of 1 May 2026" ([Baker Tilly](https://bakertilly.be/nl/news/nieuwe-inhoudingsplicht-vanaf-1-mei-2026/)), and "first effective withholdings possible from 30 October 2026" ([Jubel](https://www.jubel.be/nieuwe-inhoudingsplicht-voor-zelfstandigen-in-de-bouw)). Plausible reading: law 1 Jan 2026, operational 1 May 2026, first debts flagged late Oct 2026 — unresolved.
- RSVZ debt threshold: sources give EUR 2,647 (2026 amount, Jubel), EUR 2,591.36 ([Unizo](https://www.unizo.be/ondernemersgids/bedrijfsvoering/samenwerken-met-zelfstandigen/werken-met-dienstverleners)) and EUR 2,500 (Forum for the Future) — likely the same indexed base amount in different years; verify.
- Sanction for not withholding (RSVZ regime as reported): fine of 15% of the invoice amount excl. VAT, max EUR 10,578.23 (2026) — search extract of [Securex](https://www.securex.be/nl/lex4you/zelfstandige/nieuws/inhoudingsplicht-in-de-bouw-en-schoonmaaksector-vanaf-2026) / [Jubel](https://www.jubel.be/nieuwe-inhoudingsplicht-voor-zelfstandigen-in-de-bouw)
- The obligation covers opdrachtgevers and contractors paying (sub)contractors in construction and cleaning; private persons acting purely privately are excluded — [VLAIO](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/nieuws/inhoudingsplicht-uitgebreid-wat-moet-jij-als-opdrachtgever-doen), [Unizo](https://www.unizo.be/ondernemersgids/bedrijfsvoering/samenwerken-met-zelfstandigen/werken-met-dienstverleners)
- Delivery of ready-mix concrete was brought within "werken in onroerende staat" (title of Schoups article) — relevant because payments to the betoncentrale then fall under the check — [Schoups via legalnews](https://legalnews.be/arbeid-sociale-zekerheid/leveren-van-stortklaar-beton-wordt-een-werk-in-onroerende-staat-schoups/)

### Inferences
- Software requirements (aankopen/betalingen): flag supplier as "30bis-relevant" (works in immovable state incl. ready-mix concrete delivery, subcontractors, self-employed helpers); at payment-proposal time run a debt check per supplier (KBO number) through the webservice or, as fallback, deep-link to checkinhoudingsplicht.be and let the user record the result; store result + timestamp + proof (PDF/response) per payment for audit; split the payment into: net to supplier, 35% to RSZ, 15% to fiscus, 15% to RSVZ (capped at 50% total), each with its own structured payment reference/IBAN; generate the SEPA lines; book the withheld amounts as payment of the invoice; give the subcontractor a statement.
- Because the percentages apply to the amount excl. VAT and interact with reverse charge (most B2B subcontract invoices have 0 VAT), the calculation base must be explicit in the UI.
- For outgoing invoices: show the contractor's own status (customers will check us) — a dashboard widget "own inhoudingsplicht status" prevents surprises.
- Webservice access to social-security services generally requires a certificate and technical user (see section 4), which is a per-company onboarding step; alternatively the vendor acts as a service provider. This needs a technical feasibility check.

- Technical spec of BillRetainment (SOAP/REST, auth, quotas, whether third-party SaaS may call on behalf of customers) not retrieved — socialsecurity.be must be opened.
- Fiscal side (art. 403 WIB92): check via MyMinfin / the combined site, payment procedure and the hoofdelijke aansprakelijkheid caps (100% of contract price for RSZ, 35% for tax — background knowledge, unverified) not sourced here.
- The exception for invoices below EUR 7,143 with a recent attest (background knowledge) not sourced.

## 4. Checkinatwork / Check In and Out at Work, Aangifte van werken, Limosa, ConstruBadge, new rules

### Takeaway
Aangifte van werken (30bis) is required before start for works >= EUR 30,000 excl. VAT or >= EUR 5,000 when a subcontractor is used; attendance registration applies on sites >= EUR 500,000 excl. VAT. From 1 April 2027 Checkinatwork becomes "Check In and Out at Work": arrival AND departure times, no more remote pre-registration — a direct functional driver for a site/time-registration app.

### Cited Findings
- Attendance registration threshold EUR 500,000 excl. VAT, applicable since 1 March 2016 (KB 15 Feb 2016) — [FOD WASO](https://werk.belgie.be/nl/news/aanwezigheidsregistratie-op-bouwplaatsen-verlaagd-drempelbedrag-vanaf-1-maart-2016)
- Sites subject to Checkinatwork must be declared in the Aangifte van werken; registration channels include an online service, mobile app, gateway and a webservice for own registration systems; webservice use requires an own registration system, a GlobalSign certificate and creation of a technical user; specs in the socialsecurity.be webservice catalogue — [socialsecurity.be Checkinatwork](https://www.socialsecurity.be/site_nl/employer/applics/checkinatwork/index.htm), [FAQ PDF](https://www.socialsecurity.be/site_nl/employer/applics/checkinatwork/documents/pdf/vragen_aanwezigheidsregistratie_NL.pdf)
- From 1 April 2027 (postponed from 1 Jan 2027) Checkinatwork is replaced by Check In and Out at Work for works in immovable state: both arrival and departure time on the temporary or mobile site must be registered; presence can no longer be registered in advance and remotely; EUR 500,000 threshold kept; legal basis Programmawet 30 May 2026 (BS 1 June 2026) and RSZ communication of 30 July 2026 — [Partena](https://www.partena-professional.be/nl/onze-inzichten/infoflashes/check-and-out-work-voor-werken-onroerende-staat-uitgesteld-naar-1-april), [Lexgo](https://www.lexgo.be/nl/nieuws-en-publicaties/15016-actief-op-belgische-werven-vanaf-2027-wordt-in-en-uitchecken-verplicht), [Securex](https://www.securex.be/nl/lex4you/werkgever/nieuws/naar-een-verplichte-check-in-en-out-in-de-bouw-vanaf-2027)
- The employer is not obliged to give workers a smartphone for registration; a draft KB went to the Hoge Raad for advice in April 2026 — [Hoge Raad advies 281](https://werk.belgie.be/sites/default/files/content/documents/Welzijn%20op%20het%20werk/Adviezen%20Hoge%20Raad/Adv281_D299_Checkout_Bouw.pdf), [Securex](https://www.securex.be/nl/lex4you/werkgever/nieuws/naar-een-verplichte-check-in-en-out-in-de-bouw-vanaf-2027)
- Check In and Out at Work already applies to certain cleaning activities since September 2024 — [Partena](https://www.partena-professional.be/nl/onze-inzichten/infoflashes/check-and-out-work-voor-werken-onroerende-staat-uitgesteld-naar-1-april)
- Aangifte van werken thresholds: without subcontractor mandatory from EUR 30,000 excl. VAT (previously 25,000); with one subcontractor from EUR 5,000 excl. VAT; declaration before start of works, mandatory electronically via the application Aangifte van werken (art. 30bis and 30ter wet 27/06/1969); by the contractor engaged by the opdrachtgever; subcontractors (also foreign) must be added — [Securex](https://www.securex.be/nl/lex4you/werkgever/themas/verplichtingen-werkgever/verplichtingen-in-bepaalde-sectoren/welke-werken-moeten-aangegeven-worden), [socialsecurity.be FAQ AvW](https://www.socialsecurity.be/site_nl/employer/applics/ddt/general/faq.htm)
- With two or more subcontractors the sources/tables are inconsistent on whether any amount triggers the declaration — [socialsecurity.be FAQ AvW](https://www.socialsecurity.be/site_nl/employer/applics/ddt/general/faq.htm)
- Without a werfmelding no Checkinatwork registration is possible — [Crivits & Persyn via legalnews](https://legalnews.be/arbeid-sociale-zekerheid/unieke-werfmelding-en-checkinwork-een-gewaarschuwd-aannemer-is-er-twee-waard-crivits-persyn/)
- Flanders: from 2026 a "zorgvuldigheidsplicht" for contractors against illegal employment in the chain (same exceptions as aangifte van werken) — [Partena](https://www.partena-professional.be/nl/node/22078)
- General working-time registration for all employers is announced for 1 Jan 2027 (budget agreement end 2025; preliminary draft by Minister Clarinval; not yet a final law as of Oct 2026; system must be objective, reliable and accessible; must capture hours actually worked, not the roster; transition to 31 March 2027) — [Partena FAQ](https://www.partena-professional.be/nl/node/22640), [Securex](https://www.securex.be/nl/lex4you/werkgever/nieuws/tijdsregistratie-vanaf-2027-waar-staan-we-vandaag), [Deloitte](https://www.deloitte.com/be/en/services/tax/blogs/tax-legal-pulse-nl/verplichte-tijdsregistratie-vanaf-2027-wat-betekent-dit-voor-uw-onderneming.html)

### Inferences
- Project master data must hold: total contract value excl. VAT (drives 30k/5k/500k thresholds), werfmeldingsnummer (aangifte van werken), list of subcontractors per site with KBO number, start/end dates, and flags "AvW required", "CIAO required".
- Minimum feature: reminders/blocks ("site value >= 30,000 and no werfmeldingsnummer", "subcontractor added but not declared"). Stretch feature: push check-in/check-out records from the mobile time-registration app to the RSZ webservice per worker (INSZ/Limosa number) per site per day; from April 2027 on-site, real-time check-in and check-out (geo-fenced or QR at site) becomes legally relevant, which aligns with the general working-time registration expected in 2027 — one clocking action could serve payroll hours, nacalculatie and CIAO.
- Worker master data needed: INSZ (rijksregisternummer) or Limosa ID, employer KBO, statute (arbeider/bediende/zelfstandige/interim/foreign posted).
- Since specs for the 2027 CIAO webservice are still being finalised, plan the integration as a pluggable connector and budget time in Q1 2027.

### Gaps
- Limosa (declaration of posted foreign workers/self-employed, L1 document to be checked by the user of the services) and ConstruBadge (sector ID badge issued by Constructiv) — no sources retrieved; background knowledge, unverified.
- Sanction levels for missing AvW (5% of undeclared amount — background knowledge) and missing check-in not sourced.

## 5. VAT: btw verlegd (medecontractant), 6% renovation, 6% sloop & heropbouw, invoice mentions, credit notes

### Takeaway
The invoice engine needs at least four construction VAT regimes: 21% standard, reverse charge "btw verlegd" (art. 20 KB nr. 1) for customers filing periodic VAT returns, 6% renovation of dwellings >= 10 years with a mandatory declaration text on the invoice (attest abolished 2022), and 6% demolition-and-rebuild under the permanent regime since 1 July 2025 with prior MyMinfin declaration (forms 111).

### Cited Findings
- Reverse charge: if the customer is a VAT taxable person filing periodic returns, VAT on works in immovable state is shifted (art. 20 §1 KB nr. 1); the contractor charges no VAT and mentions "btw verlegd"; a change effective 1 Jan 2023 concerns the required wording (formerly the short mention "Btw verlegd volgens art. 20 KB nr. 1" sufficed) — [Accountable help](https://www.accountable.eu/nl-be/help-center/het-btw-medecontractantstelsel-in-belgie-wanneer-en-hoe-verlegging-van-heffing-van-toepassing-is-op-bouwwerken/), [I AM D help](https://intercom.help/iamd/nl/articles/9677440-btw-vermeldingen-op-je-facturen)
- 6% renovation: only for works on dwellings at least 10 years old used exclusively or mainly as private dwelling — [Securex](https://www.securex.be/nl/lex4you/zelfstandige/nieuws/btw-van-6-voor-de-renovatie-van-woningen), [Wolters Kluwer](https://www.wolterskluwer.com/nl-be/expert-insights/vat-rate-of-6-pct-for-work-in-immovable-property)
- The customer's VAT attest was replaced by a statutory declaration on the invoice (wet 27 Dec 2021); transition: attest still usable until 30 June 2022, invoice mention mandatory from 1 July 2022 — [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/be/nl/home/insights/2022/02/kmo-renovatie-en-herstellingswerken-afschaffing-btw-attest.html), [Baker Tilly](https://bakertilly.be/nl/news/renovatie-woningen-aan-6-btw-niet-langer-attest-nodig/), [Aternio via legalnews](https://legalnews.be/bouw-vastgoed/renovatie-aan-6-btw-verklaring-op-factuur-vervangt-attest-aternio/)
- 6% and reverse charge exclude each other in the typical case (private dwelling 6% with declaration versus B2B reverse charge) per the secondary summary — [Accountable](https://www.accountable.eu/nl-be/blog/btw-tarieven-bouw/)
- Demolition and rebuild: permanent nationwide 6% regime from 1 July 2025 for private persons and developers, incl. sale on plan; enacted by law of 29 July 2025 with administrative tolerance from 1 July 2025; circular 2025/C/48 — [Forum for the Future / circular 2025/C/48](https://blog.forumforthefuture.be/nl/article/circulaire-2025c48-betreffende-de-nieuwe-regeling-inzake-het-btw-tarief-van-6-voor-de-afbraak-en-heropbouw-van-woningen/28054), [Lydian via legalnews](https://legalnews.be/bouw-vastgoed/nieuw-veralgemeend-6-btw-tarief-bij-afbraak-en-heropbouw-lydian/), [Vlaanderen.be](https://www.vlaanderen.be/btw-tarief-van-6-bij-renovatie-van-woningen)
- Prior declaration via MyMinfin with forms 111/1 (own occupation), 111/5 (rental to private person), 111/2 (social rental), 111/3 (supply of rebuilt dwelling); a copy must be given to the contractor(s)/medecontractant — [Forum for the Future, forms 111](https://blog.forumforthefuture.be/nl/article/nieuwe-formulieren-111-voor-definitieve-regeling-afbraak-wederopbouw-beschikbaar/29146), [DVD Tax Law via legalnews](https://legalnews.be/bouw-vastgoed/afbraak-en-heropbouw-de-nieuwe-formulieren-111-zijn-beschikbaar-dvd-tax-law/)
- Tolerance for missing declarations ran until 31 Dec 2025 with filing by 31 Jan 2026 (now expired) — [Forum for the Future](https://blog.forumforthefuture.be/nl/article/nieuwe-formulieren-111-voor-definitieve-regeling-afbraak-wederopbouw-beschikbaar/29146)
- Fossil-fuel heating installations are again at 21% since July 2025 (programme law VAT measures) — [Jubel](https://www.jubel.be/welke-btw-maatregelen-bracht-de-programmawet/)
- SBB overview of VAT novelties for the construction sector — [SBB](https://www.sbb.be/nl/node/3913)

### Inferences
- Per project (not per customer) store: VAT regime, building age declaration (first use >= 10 years), dwelling use, sloop-heropbouw declaration reference/date and copy of form 111 as attachment; block 6% sloop-heropbouw invoicing when no declaration copy is on file.
- Invoice templates must insert the correct statutory text automatically (reverse-charge text; the long 6% declaration paragraph including the customer's one-month objection wording), and the UBL must carry the right tax category code and exemption reason. The exact legal wording must be copied from the FOD Financiën text — not retrieved here.
- Mixed invoices occur (e.g. 6% works plus 21% fossil boiler); VAT must be line-level.
- For a concrete subcontractor almost all sales are B2B reverse charge; purchases of ready-mix concrete with placement may also be reverse charge (works in immovable state) while pure supply of goods (rebar, formwork rental) is 21% — the purchase side needs both regimes.
- Credit notes: must reference the original invoice, also go via Peppol for B2B, and reverse the same VAT regime; vorderingsstaat corrections are commonly handled in the next cumulative state rather than by credit note — configurable.

### Gaps
- Exact current statutory invoice texts (reverse charge and 6%) and credit-note rules (art. 54 WBTW / KB nr. 1 art. 12, mention "btw terug te storten aan de Staat...") not sourced — background knowledge, unverified.
- Conditions of the 2025 sloop-heropbouw regime (max 175 m2 habitable area, only and own dwelling, 5 years) not confirmed by extract.
- General list of mandatory invoice mentions (art. 5 KB nr. 1) and retention periods (generally 10 years for invoices/accounting — background knowledge) not sourced.

## 6. Other: Wet Breyne, ten-year liability insurance, late payment, PC 124 time/pay rules, GDPR/geolocation

### Takeaway
Wet Breyne constrains invoicing for turnkey/on-plan housing (max 5% advance, instalments never above executed value, guarantee 5% or 100%); ten-year liability insurance is mandatory since 1 July 2018 for dwellings requiring an architect; PC 124 brings sector-specific pay elements (mobility allowance, weather unemployment with electronic C3.2A) that a time-registration module must feed to the payroll provider; geolocation of workers is allowed only with a defined legal basis, clear purpose, proportionality and a transparent policy.

### Cited Findings
- Wet Breyne: advance at signing max 5% of total price; instalments may not exceed the value of works already executed; provisional and final acceptance mandatory with at least one year between; erkende aannemer gives a 5% guarantee released in halves at provisional/final acceptance, non-erkende a 100% completion or repayment guarantee — [Bouwunie via Bouwinfo](https://www.bouwinfo.be/news/bouwen-en-verbouwen/wetgeving-en-premies/partner/b2b/bouwunie/de-wet-breyne-wat-moet-je-weten-als-aannemer), [Notaris.be](https://www.notaire.be/node/281)
- The EU Court of Justice ruled against the differentiated guarantee regime; equalising at 5% is seen as likely; extension of Wet Breyne to renovation and casco projects is planned in the 2025-2029 legislature — [Schoups via legalnews](https://legalnews.be/bouw-vastgoed/het-hof-van-justitie-fluit-de-belgische-wet-breyne-terug-schoups/), [Caluwaerts Uytterhoeven via legalnews](https://legalnews.be/bouw-vastgoed/uitbreiding-van-de-wet-breyne-naar-renovaties-in-legislatuur-2025-2029-caluwaerts-uytterhoeven/)
- Law of 31 May 2017 (BS 9 June 2017): mandatory insurance of ten-year civil liability for contractors, architects and other service providers for works on dwellings requiring an architect; mandatory from 1 July 2018; annual policy or per-site policy — [Odigo via legalnews](https://legalnews.be/bouw-vastgoed/verplichte-verzekering-voor-tienjarige-aansprakelijkheid-vanaf-1-juli-2018-odigo-advocaten/), [Lexgo](https://www.lexgo.be/en/news-and-articles/4636-woningbouw-aansprakelijkheidsverzekering-wordt-ook-verplicht-voor-aannemers-en-andere-dienstverleners)
- Late payment law (2 Aug 2002, amended, effective 1 Feb 2022): max 60 days B2B incl. verification; EUR 40 flat fee — [Credendo](https://credendo.com/nl/knowledge-hub/aangepaste-betalingstermijnen-vanaf-1-februari-2022)
- PC 124: mobility allowance amounts unchanged for 2026; one day mobility leave when the worker drove 28,500 km or more in the previous year (was 30,000), paid as a public holiday; sector agreement 2025-2026 concluded; sector ecocheque scheme stopped 31 March 2026 — [Securex mobiliteitsverlof](https://www.securex.be/nl/lex4you/werkgever/nieuws/denk-aan-het-mobiliteitsverlof-pc-s-124-en-149-010), [Partena sectorakkoord](https://www.partena-professional.be/nl/onze-inzichten/infoflashes/sectorakkoord-bereikt-binnen-pc-124-bouw)
- Weather unemployment (weerverlet): only for arbeiders; weather must make work directly impossible; employer must notify the RVA (first unemployment day of each month, with precise circumstances); construction workers use control card C3.2A-BOUW (numbered, issued monthly via Constructiv); paper C3.2A abolished from 1 Jan 2025 in favour of the electronic eC3.2, with a transition regime for construction — [Securex](https://www.securex.be/nl/lex4you/werkgever/nieuws/tijdelijke-werkloosheid-omwille-van-slecht-weer-vergeet-de-aangifte-niet), [Forum for the Future](https://blog.forumforthefuture.be/nl/article/tijdelijke-werkloosheid-wegens-slecht-weer-extra-informatie-vereist-door-de-rva/29693), [Accent webinar eC3.2](https://accentjobs.be/assets/cms/2024-11/Presentatie_webinar-Wegwijs_met_de_elektronische_stempelkaart.pdf)
- Geolocation: no specific statute; GBA found a breach where the employer had defined no legal basis; geolocation can be justified as necessary for the business so consent is not the basis; control of task execution must be targeted and proportionate; a clear geolocation policy is required (work-rules mention alone is insufficient); letting employees switch tracking on/off is cited as good practice — [Claeys & Engels via legalnews](https://legalnews.be/arbeid-sociale-zekerheid/geolocalisatiesysteem-in-voertuigen-van-werknemers-gba-brengt-voorwaarden-in-herinnering-voor-legitiem-gebruik-claeys-engels/), [Securex](https://www.securex.be/nl/lex4you/werkgever/nieuws/gebruik-je-een-track-trace-systeem-check-je-geolokalisatiebeleid), [Lexgo](https://www.lexgo.be/nl/nieuws-en-publicaties/14141-geolocalisatie-van-bedrijfswagens-bevestiging-van-de-principes-van-de-belgische-gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit), [PwC Legal](https://www.pwclegal.be/en/news/geolocation-technology-in-company-cars--how-to-get-on-the-right-.html)

### Inferences
- Wet Breyne mode on a project: block advance invoices > 5%, validate each instalment against executed value (link to vorderingsstaat), track guarantee and both opleveringen. Only relevant for contractors selling directly to private builders; a concrete subcontractor rarely needs it, so make it optional.
- Insurance: store policy (insurer, number, annual or per-site) and attach attest to offertes/contracts for dwelling projects; expiry reminders.
- Time registration should capture per worker per day per site: start/stop, breaks, travel (km driver/passenger for mobility allowance), absence codes (weerverlet, vorst, rustdag, inhaalrust, feestdag, ziekte), and export to social secretariats (SD Worx, Securex, Liantis, Partena, Acerta) — do not attempt payroll calculation. Hours also feed nacalculatie at site/post level.
- GDPR design: geo-capture only at clock-in/clock-out events rather than continuous tracking; purpose limitation documented; privacy policy template for the customer; role-based access; retention limits; DPA with customers (vendor is processor); INSZ numbers are sensitive identifiers — encrypt and restrict.

### Gaps
- Not sourced: PC 124 wage scales by category (I-IV, ploegbaas), exact mobility-allowance amounts, rustdagen calendar 2026/2027, getrouwheidszegels/weerverletzegels (Constructiv), 40-hour week with 12 rest days, vorstverlet rules.

- Whether the invoice or offerte must mention the ten-year insurer; KB on alternative borgtocht — not established.

## 7. Concrete-contractor (betonbouw) domain workflow

### Takeaway
Publicly documented material covers the specification and delivery side well (NBN EN 206 + NBN B 15-001, BENOR delivery slips, site checks) but almost nothing was found on how Belgian betonaannemers actually run calculatie/nacalculatie; that part must be validated by interviews. The delivery slip (leveringsbon) of the betoncentrale is the pivotal document linking order, pour, quality and cost.

### Cited Findings
- Concrete is specified under NBN EN 206 with Belgian supplement NBN B 15-001; basic data: strength class (A), field of use (B1: unreinforced/reinforced/prestressed), environment class(es) (B2), plus consistency and maximum aggregate size; the standard links environment class to minimum strength class — [Buildwise folder specificatie beton](https://www.buildwise.be/media/yjwl0ywi/folder_specificatie_van_beton_volgens_de_normen_nbn_en_206_en_nbn_b_15_001.pdf), [Buildwise fiches hulp bij specificatie](https://www.buildwise.be/media/nzahvftc/na_fiches_hulp_bij_de_specificatie_van_beton-1.pdf)
- A new NBN B 15-001 was published summer 2022 (adds the option of prescribing consistency); Buildwise released a concrete app for specification — [Buildwise/Embuild press release](https://embuild.be/sites/default/files/files/2023-04/Buildwise_beton-app-1.1_persbericht_NL.pdf)
- The NBN portal lists NBN B 15-001:2024 (national supplement to NBN EN 206:2013+A2:2021), published 11 July 2024 — so the 2022 edition appears superseded — [NBN portal](https://app.nbn.be/data/r/platform/public-portal/home?p_lang=nl)
- Specifications must be put in the bestek and the order form; at delivery, the specification is checked against the leveringsbon — [Buildwise onepager beton](https://www.buildwise.be/media/shdc0q0x/onepager_beton_01_05_nl.pdf)
- BENOR delivery slip: BENOR logo plus category (S or D); guaranteed workability time is stated (typically 100 min with CEM I, 120 min with CEM III); BENOR mark is lost if water, fibres or admixtures are added on site at the customer's initiative — [Holcim BENOR fiche](https://www.holcim.be/sites/belgium/files/documents/fiche_technique_benor_beton_nl_lr.pdf), [Holcim infofiche 5](https://www.holcim.be/sites/belgium/files/documents/triptyque_infofiche5_nl_lr.pdf)
- Supplier terms: the customer must check the delivery slip and report deviations immediately — [Heidelberg Materials algemene verkoopvoorwaarden beton België](https://www.heidelbergmaterials-benelux.com/sites/default/files/2023-12/Algemene%20verkoopvoorwaarden_beton_belgi%C3%AB.pdf)
- Plant logistics example: a 9 m3 truck mixer cycle limits plant output to about 60 m3/h — relevant for pour planning (Dutch teaching material) — [BBT H7](https://maken.wikiwijs.nl/bestanden/876417/BBT%20H7%20%20jan.%202019%20Vervaardigen%20van%20betonspecie.pdf)
- Delivery of ready-mix concrete is treated as work in immovable state for 30bis purposes (title-level evidence) — [Schoups via legalnews](https://legalnews.be/arbeid-sociale-zekerheid/leveren-van-stortklaar-beton-wordt-een-werk-in-onroerende-staat-schoups/)
- Buildwise offers Coplaco, a planning/cost tool importing a summary meetstaat from Excel/CSV — evidence that Excel is the common exchange medium for SME contractors — [Buildwise Coplaco handleiding](https://www.buildwise.be/media/5ghhgl1d/coplaco-handleiding.pdf)

### Inferences (domain model proposal — to validate with contractors; not sourced)
- Calculatie per structural element (funderingszool, funderingsplaat, kolom, wand, balk, vloerplaat/predal/breedplaat, trap): quantities m3 concrete (per concrete type), kg or ton rebar (often via kg/m3 ratio per element type, or from buigstaat), m2 bekisting (per system: wand, kolom, vloer/onderslag, randbekisting), m/stuks accessories (stekkenbakken, voegbanden, afstandhouders, centerpennen, ontkistingsolie), labour hours via norms (h/m2 bekisten, h/ton vlechten, h/m3 storten) x team hourly cost, pump cost (voorrijkost + hourly/m3 rate + minimum), crane/materieel, formwork rental (EUR/m2/week x duration), losses (stortverlies %), then overhead and margin. Unit prices per meetstaat post are built from these resources (recipe/"samengestelde prijs").
- Uitvoering: pour planning per stortfase (date, element, m3, concrete spec string e.g. strength class / environment class / consistency / Dmax / cement type, pump yes/no, plant, interval of trucks); order to betoncentrale (call-off against a framework price agreement); leveringsbonnen per truck (bon number, plant, time loaded/arrival/start-end unloading, m3, spec, BENOR, additions, waiting time); stortrapport (weather, temperature, element, slump/flow checks, cubes taken with IDs, curing measures, ontkistingsdatum); kubusproeven results at 7/28 days linked to pour and element; bekistingshuur (leverbon/retourbon, m2 and weeks on site, damages/missing parts); materieelbeheer (own formwork stock, props, pumps, inspections/keuringen); rebar deliveries with buigstaat and BENOR certificates.
- Nacalculatie: actual m3 from delivery slips vs theoretical m3 from meetstaat (overconsumption %), actual hours from time registration vs budget hours per activity (bekisten/vlechten/storten), rental duration vs plan, pump waiting surcharges, small-order and Saturday/winter surcharges from supplier invoices. Because supplier invoices now arrive as structured Peppol UBL, automatic three-way match (order - leveringsbon - invoice) is feasible and is a differentiator.
- Vorderingsstaat: executed quantities per post derive from poured elements (m3 poured per element marks element as done -> also its rebar kg and formwork m2), giving a near-automatic monthly state.
- Current practice (assumption): Excel calculation sheets with own norms, paper/photographed leveringsbonnen in site binders, WhatsApp for pour planning, paper werfdagboek, Excel vorderingsstaten on the architect's meetstaat layout. Needs interview validation.

### Gaps
- No reliable published source found on the day-to-day calculatie/nacalculatie practice of Belgian concrete contractors, labour norms, or what share still works in Excel/paper — interviews or Embuild/Bouwunie/FEBE contacts needed.
- Site testing regime (number of cubes per m3/pour, who samples, lab accreditation), TRA 550 (BENOR ready-mix regulation), NBN EN 13670 + NBN B 15-400 (execution), PTV rebar/BENOR steel: not retrieved.
- Pump tariffs, waiting-time surcharges and betoncentrale price-list structures: no usable source.
- Whether betoncentrales (Holcim, Heidelberg/Inter-Beton, CCB, Kijlstra etc.) offer digital delivery notes/APIs or order portals: not researched; potentially important integration.
- The exact changes in NBN B 15-001:2024 versus 2022 not read.

## 8. Standard data formats: meetstaat, bestek systems, BIM/IFC, imports

### Takeaway
There is no single Belgian exchange standard for meetstaten; in practice Excel (and PDF) dominate, with public clients each using their own bestek system and post numbering (VMSW Bouwtechnisch Bestek Woningbouw, Standaardbestek 250 with catalogue of standard posts, eDelta/eBestekmaker XML for Flemish infrastructure). Robust Excel import with column mapping is the must-have; XML formats are client-specific extras.

### Cited Findings
- Standaardbestek 250 (Flemish road works) has a catalogue of standardised posts (catalogus 4.1a) used for post numbering in meetstaten — [AWV catalogus 4.1a](https://wegenenverkeer.be/sites/default/files/uploads/documenten/Catalogus-4.1a.pdf)

- VMSW Bouwtechnisch Bestek Woningbouw (BBW): chapter/article/sub-article numbering (chapters with up to 100 articles each with up to 100 sub-articles); concordance tables between BBW versions (2015/2025) are published — [BBW handleiding](https://assets.vlaanderen.be/image/upload/v1678302305/Handleiding_BouwtechnischBestekWoningbouw_ve8she.pdf), [Vlaanderen BBW wijzigingen/concordantie](https://www.vlaanderen.be/sociaal-woonbeleid/sociale-woningen/realiseren-of-renoveren-van-sociale-woonprojecten/woningbouw-en-infrastructuur/ontwerp-en-bestek/bouwtechnisch-bestek-woningbouw/bbw-wijzigingen-2015-2025-concordantietabellen-en-overgangsfase)

### Inferences
- Import requirements: (1) Excel meetstaat import with interactive column mapping (post number, description, unit, quantity, VH/FH code, unit price, total), detection of chapter/sub-total rows, preservation of original numbering and order so that the vorderingsstaat can be exported back in the client's layout; (2) PDF meetstaat extraction (table OCR/AI) as assisted import with human review; (3) optional templates for VMSW BBW, SB250 catalogue, CCTB (Wallonia/Brussels) numbering; (4) supplier price lists via Excel/CSV import; (5) export to Excel always.
- For a concrete subcontractor the input is usually a slice of the main contractor's or architect's meetstaat (ruwbouw/beton posts) plus structural drawings and buigstaten (PDF); IFC quantity take-off is a later-stage feature — most SME concrete work is still drawn/quoted from 2D PDF (assumption, unverified).

- Any Belgian equivalent of CUF or GAEB, and the actual use of IFC/BIM quantity take-off among Belgian SME contractors (Buildwise BIM surveys) — no source retrieved.
