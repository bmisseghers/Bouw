# Competitive landscape: all-in-one construction business software in Belgium (state as of Nov 2026)

> **Method caveat (read first).** Direct page fetches were blocked in this research session (vendor sites, Capterra/Software Advice, Fortino etc. were outside the allowed network), so **every finding below comes from web-search result summaries/snippets of the cited URL, not from a full read of the page**. Prices and counts should be re-checked on the linked pages before being quoted in a final report. Many "comparison" sources are written by vendors (Robaws, Bouwflow, UNIVO, Enfin, Taklo, Prezapp) and are flagged as such. No NBB annual accounts were read directly.

## 1. Vertuoza

### Takeaway
VC-backed Walloon scale-up (EUR 10M raised, ~90-120 staff, claims 13,000+ construction businesses) with a French-language centre of gravity and expansion into FR/LU/CH/NL; it sells quote-based annual packs with no public price and no free trial. Its weak spots are contract lock-in (the dominant review complaint), middling ratings (~3.3-3.7/5), a thin mobile app, limited integrations/no documented open API, and comparatively shallow progress-claim/planning depth.

### Cited Findings
**Company facts**
- Based in Nivelles; raised EUR 4M pre-Series A in Oct 2022 led by XAnge (also seed lead) and Fortino Capital; 45 employees at that time — [Silicon Canals](https://siliconcanals.com/belgium-saas-vertuoza-bags-4m/), [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2022/10/14/belgiums-vertuoza-raises-eur4-million-in-pre-series-a/)
- Series A closed Nov 2023 with a further EUR 6M, total EUR 10M; led by Antoni Di Filippo and Dominique Pellegrino; active in Belgium, Luxembourg, France, Switzerland; Dutch version launched, Dutch market entry planned — [Fortino Capital](https://www.fortinocapital.com/news/vertuoza-reinforces-its-growth-ambitions-and-successfully-concludes-its-10-million-eu-series)
- Aggregator totals differ: CB Insights USD 11.82M over 3 rounds; Tracxn USD 5.31M over 2 rounds; Dealroom EUR 10M — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/vertuoza/financials), [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/vertuoza/__NWgymfDTszXMof0aMBZ-DcdpenB7Er4iVmu-BwSAUrg/funding-and-investors), [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/companies/vertuoza/)
- Headcount: 121 as of 31 Jul 2026 (Tracxn); 115 (PitchBook); 88 (Latka, possibly older) — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/vertuoza/__NWgymfDTszXMof0aMBZ-DcdpenB7Er4iVmu-BwSAUrg), [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/462711-97), [Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/vertuoza#team)
- Revenue: third-party estimate of USD 4M for 2024 (not an audited figure) — [Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/vertuoza#customers)
- No acquisitions made to date per Tracxn — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/vertuoza/__NWgymfDTszXMof0aMBZ-DcdpenB7Er4iVmu-BwSAUrg)
- Vendor claims 13,000+ construction businesses use it daily — [Vertuoza NL-BE site](https://www.vertuoza.com/nl-be); an older award page cited 7,000+ users in 5 countries — [Belgian Construction Awards](https://belgianconstructionawards.be/vertuoza/)

**Segment / positioning**
- Targets teams of roughly 5 to 50 employees; also pitched as an entry model for starters — [Vertuoza software-bouw page](https://www.vertuoza.com/nl-be/saas/software-bouw), [Vertuoza offerte-facturatie page](https://www.vertuoza.com/nl-be/saas/offerte-facturatie-bouw)
- Described as best suited to small/mid-sized builders wanting to digitise fast without month-long implementations — [Bedrijfssoftwaregids](https://bedrijfssoftwaregids.nl/blog/projectmanagement-software-bouwbedrijven-2026/)

**Features**
- Import of architect's Excel bill of quantities (meetstaat) matched to an integrated price library; reported to be part of the "Expert" pack — [Vertuoza price library](https://www.vertuoza.com/fr-be/fonctionnalites/bibliotheque-de-prix), [Le Conduc](https://leconduc.fr/logiciel-batiment/logiciel-gestion-chantier/avis-vertuoza/)
- Gantt planning, weather, time clocking (pointage) and route logs; clocking is tied to planning — [Vertuoza planning](https://www.vertuoza.com/fr-fr/fonctionnalites/le-planning)
- Detailed clocking, absences, HR and mobility are reserved for higher packs (Expert+) — [Le Conduc](https://leconduc.fr/logiciel-batiment/logiciel-gestion-chantier/avis-vertuoza/), [Skello](https://www.skello.io/blog/avis-vertuoza-alternatives)
- Handles advances and progress claims (voorschotten, vorderingsstaten), progress invoices and change orders — [Vertuoza offerte-facturatie page](https://www.vertuoza.com/nl-be/saas/offerte-facturatie-bouw)
- Connected to Peppol — [Vertuoza Peppol blog](https://www.vertuoza.com/fr-fr/blog/tout-comprendre-sur-le-systeme-peppol)
- AI: voice-driven quote assistant "VertuoAI"; AI-created supplier invoices appear in the help center — [Vertuoza help center](https://intercom-help.eu/vertuoza/nl/articles/431299-waarom-worden-sommige-door-ai-aangemaakte-leveranciersfacturen-niet-bovenaan-de-lijst-weergegeven), [Cobria comparison](https://www.cobria.com/en/articles/cobria-vs-vertuoza)
- "VertuoWork" private construction marketplace — [Vertuoza VertuoWork](https://www.vertuoza.com/nl-nl/functies/vertuowork)
- Integrations page exists, but a reviewer reports a limited number of native integrations and no documented open API or Zapier connector — [Vertuoza integrations](https://www.vertuoza.com/nl-be/integrations), [Le Conduc](https://leconduc.fr/logiciel-batiment/logiciel-gestion-chantier/avis-vertuoza/)
- Competitor claim (Robaws, not neutral): Vertuoza lacks quote parameters, progress claims ("vorderingen"), webshop/wholesaler links, automation workflows and custom fields — [Robaws vs Vertuoza](https://robaws.com/nl-BE/vergelijking-robaws-vs-vertuoza); this conflicts with Vertuoza's own claim to handle vorderingsstaten (above)

**Pricing**
- No public price; price "depends on business needs", demo required — [Taklo](https://taklo.nl/beste/software-voor-belgische-installatiebedrijven), [Robaws vs Vertuoza](https://robaws.com/nl-BE/vergelijking-robaws-vs-vertuoza)
- Third-party figures conflict: EUR 1,490 per year per feature (Capterra/GetApp) — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/232769/Vertuoza/); "from EUR 1,320 per month" — [comparatif-logiciels](https://www.comparatif-logiciels.fr/logiciel/avis-vertuoza/); EUR 100-150 per user per month (estimate from a competitor selling custom software) — [Prezapp](https://prezapp.be/blog/vertuoza-prix-avis-alternative/)
- Annual commitment paid upfront; no free trial (1-2 h demo instead); support and storage included — [Le Conduc](https://leconduc.fr/logiciel-batiment/logiciel-gestion-chantier/avis-vertuoza/)

**Customer sentiment**
- Capterra: 3.6/5 from 22 reviews, ease of use 3.9 (updated 3 Jun 2026) — [Capterra reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/232769/Vertuoza/reviews/)
- Most frequent complaint is lock-in: withdrawal refused two days after access opened with 12 months charged; a reported EUR 3,000 loss; a Trustpilot reviewer billed a further full year because of poorly visible termination terms — [Capterra reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/232769/Vertuoza/reviews/), [Trustpilot FR](https://fr.trustpilot.com/review/vertuoza.com)
- Other complaints: many bugs; management-side mobile app gives access to almost nothing except voice quotes; invoice handling without automatic reminders; support overloaded / replies in 24h minimum / "much too slow" — [Capterra reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/232769/Vertuoza/reviews/), [Trustpilot FR](https://fr.trustpilot.com/review/vertuoza.com)
- "Can feel unpolished and somewhat amateurish" — [SoftwareFinder](https://softwarefinder.com/construction/vertuoza/reviews)
- Gantt planning judged basic versus dedicated tools — [Skello](https://www.skello.io/blog/avis-vertuoza-alternatives)
- Praised: ease of use, estimating and planning functions — [La Fabrique du Net](https://www.lafabriquedunet.fr/logiciel/vertuoza)

### Inferences
- Vertuoza's VC funding and 100+ headcount mean it will outspend any solo entrant on marketing; competing on breadth is unrealistic, competing on a narrow vertical and flexible contract terms is plausible.
- The lock-in complaints and opaque pricing are an exploitable opening: monthly cancellable terms and transparent pricing would be a visible differentiator.
- Vertuoza's origin and review base are heavily francophone; its Flemish footprint is likely thinner than Robaws/Bouwsoft (not directly verified).

### Gaps
- No verified security/ISO 27001/hosting statement found for Vertuoza.
- No confirmation of Checkinatwork, Winbooks/Octopus/Exact connectors or a client portal (searches returned nothing; check the integrations page).
- No reliable revenue (NBB accounts not consulted), no evidence of price increases, no 2025-2026 funding round found.
- Whether the 13,000 figure is companies or users, and the Belgian share, is unknown.

## 2. Robaws

### Takeaway
Bootstrapped West-Flemish cloud ERP (Zwevegem, founder Michaël Van Robaeys, ~50% yearly growth without investors) and the only main incumbent with a fully public price list: EUR 29/59/119 per month per tier with one user included. It is the functional benchmark (vorderingsstaten, nacalculatie, warehouse, 200+ integrations, REST API, own Peppol access point, MCP link to ChatGPT/Claude), but the construction-specific depth sits in the EUR 119 Pro tier and public independent reviews are sparse.

### Cited Findings
**Company facts**
- Founded by Michaël Van Robaeys; HQ Zwevegem; grows ~50% per year without investors; "more than 50 employees"; "tens of thousands of users"; as many customers outside Belgium as inside — [Made in West-Vlaanderen](https://www.made-in.be/west-vlaanderen/zwevegemse-robaws-groeit-50-procent-per-jaar-zonder-investeerders/)
- Founding year conflicts: 2018 (Made in), 2017 (GoodFirms), 2015 and "built by Eforge BV" (Embedded Finance Index) — [Made in](https://www.made-in.be/west-vlaanderen/zwevegemse-robaws-groeit-50-procent-per-jaar-zonder-investeerders/), [GoodFirms](https://www.goodfirms.co/software/robaws), [Embedded Finance Index](https://www.embeddedfinanceindex.com/companies/robaws)
- 20,000+ users in Belgium, Netherlands, UK/Ireland, Germany — [Embedded Finance Index](https://www.embeddedfinanceindex.com/companies/robaws)
- Headcount estimates vary: 26 (PitchBook), 32 (Growjo), 11-50 (LinkedIn/Crunchbase) versus 50+ (Made in) — [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/529670-35), [Growjo](https://growjo.com/company/Robaws), [LinkedIn](https://be.linkedin.com/company/robaws)
- Revenue: Growjo estimate USD 4.1M per year (estimate, not published accounts) — [Growjo](https://growjo.com/company/Robaws)
- Ranked 310th in FT 1000 Europe's fastest-growing companies (Mar 2024); Deloitte Technology Fast 50 enterprise-software winner 2022 — [Robaws blog FT](https://robaws.com/en/blog/robaws-ranks-310th-top-1000-fastest-growing-companies-europe-according-financial-times), [Robaws blog Deloitte](https://robaws.com/en/blog/robaws-has-won-2022-deloitte-technology-fast-50-award-enterprise-software-sector)

**Segment / positioning**
- "#1 cloud ERP for construction & installation"; operates in Belgium and the Netherlands mainly for SMEs; from roofers to garden contractors — [Robaws](https://robaws.com/en), [Computable.be press release](https://www.computable.be/persberichten/de-10-beste-software-voor-bouwbedrijven-in-belgie/)

**Features**
- CRM, estimates, takeoffs, quotations, calculation, invoicing, workflow automation, 200+ integrations, custom fields, Peppol compliant — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10009572/Robaws/)
- Vorderingsstaten created from the quote, cumulative executed/remaining per term, one-click invoice after approval; vorderingsstaten, nacalculatie, warehouse management and purchase invoices are in the Pro pack — [Softwareshaker (Robaws partner)](https://softwareshaker.com/functies/vorderingsstaat/), [Softwareshaker](https://softwareshaker.com/bouwbedrijven/)
- Recognised Peppol access point; sends directly via Peppol; Peppol free and unlimited from Starter — [Softwareshaker](https://softwareshaker.com/bouwbedrijven/)
- Accounting links: Exact Online, Snelstart, Twinfield, Yuki; also DATEV, QuickBooks, Xero; REST API with documentation — [Softwareshaker](https://softwareshaker.com/bouwbedrijven/), [Robaws](https://robaws.com/en)
- AI: since 10 Nov 2026 Robaws can be linked to ChatGPT or Claude via MCP to query customers, quotes, projects, invoices in natural language — [Robaws product updates 2026](https://support.robaws.com/nl/article/product-updates-2026-mch86v/)
- Mobile field app "Wappy" with separate licences — [Robaws support: Wappy licences](https://support.robaws.com/en/article/wappy-licences-c9uch7/)

**Pricing**
- Starter EUR 29/month (1 user included, extra users EUR 24); Go EUR 59 (extra users EUR 49); Pro EUR 119 (extra users EUR 89) — [Robaws pricing](https://robaws.com/en/pricing)
- Those are annual-billing prices; monthly billing is EUR 39 / 69 / 139 (checked 2 Oct 2026 by a third party) — [Bedrijfssoftwaregids](https://bedrijfssoftwaregids.nl/blog/projectmanagement-software-bouwbedrijven-2026/)
- Licences are per user, payable monthly or yearly — [Robaws support: billing](https://support.robaws.com/en/article/billing-1cnkh34/)
- Aggregators show inconsistent/older numbers (OMR: Go EUR 99, Pro EUR 199; Tekpon: 59/119/239; GetApp: from EUR 49 incl. 2 users) — [OMR](https://omr.com/en/reviews/product/robaws/pricing), [Tekpon](https://tekpon.com/software/robaws/reviews/), [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/construction-software/a/robaws/)

**Security**
- SLA page lists infrastructure as Tier III, ISO/IEC 27001, hosted in Europe, realtime replication to a second data centre, yearly external penetration tests — [Robaws SLA](https://robaws.com/en/service-level-agreement)

**Sentiment**
- G2: too few reviews for insight; three positive reviews; Software Advice/GetApp show none — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/robaws/reviews)
- OMR (German users): a long-standing display bug not fixed; no distinction between invoice types — [OMR](https://omr.com/en/reviews/product/robaws)
- Third-party criticism: limited scalability for complex operations; possible customisation gaps for complex workflows — [SpotSaaS](https://www.spotsaas.com/product/robaws)
- Enfin (competitor) publishes a "replace Robaws" comparison — [Enfin](https://enfin.be/en/blog/best-construction-software-belgium)

### Inferences
- ISO/IEC 27001 on the SLA page appears to describe the data-centre infrastructure, not necessarily a Robaws company certification; treat as unverified for the company ISMS.
- Realistic cost for a small concrete contractor needing vorderingsstaten + nacalculatie: Pro, e.g. 5 office users ≈ EUR 119 + 4 × 89 = EUR 475/month (annual billing), plus Wappy licences for site workers (price not found).
- Robaws is horizontal across trades (installers, roofers, gardeners); nothing found suggesting concrete-specific functionality. Its open API/MCP makes it also a possible platform to build on rather than compete with.

### Gaps
- No verified customer count (companies), no NBB figures read (entity appears to be Eforge BV — unverified).
- Checkinatwork and Octopus/Winbooks links not confirmed.
- Wappy licence price, onboarding/implementation fees and contract notice terms not retrieved.
- Little independent customer-review evidence either way.

## 3. Bouwflow (NWN Software)

### Takeaway
A very young (company founded Sept 2023), tiny team from Oudenaarde (register: ~1 FTE; LinkedIn 2-10) that nonetheless markets a broad construction ERP and claims 1,000+ construction professionals; entry price "from EUR 37 per user/month" plus a custom start-up fee. It is the proof that a small team can enter this market, and also the most directly comparable rival for a new solo entrant.

### Cited Findings
- Developed by NWN Software; described as an all-encompassing ERP for the Belgian construction and installation sector — [NWN Software](https://www.nwnsoftware.be/), [NWN Bouwflow page](https://www.nwnsoftware.be/diensten/bouwflow)
- NWN Software (Oudenaarde, BE 0805.950.036) founded 18 Sep 2023; register reports 1 FTE in latest financial statements — [Companyweb](https://www.companyweb.be/en/0805950036/nwn-software); LinkedIn lists 2-10 employees — [LinkedIn](https://be.linkedin.com/company/nwn-software)
- Co-founders per LinkedIn: Wannes Matthys, Nicholas Martens (also freelance full-stack developer), Noah Geeraerts; claim of 1,000+ construction professionals using the software (self-reported) — [LinkedIn Wannes Matthys](https://www.linkedin.com/in/wannesmatthys/)
- Target: construction companies with 5 to 100 employees; finishing companies, residential builders, installers, contractors — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/bouwflow/reviews), [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10026884/Bouwflow/)
- Features: project management, planning, pre- and post-calculation, progress claims; phased invoicing (advance, interim, final) and sending via Peppol; accounting integrations incl. Billit — [Bouwflow features](https://www.bouwflow.be/en/features), [Bouwflow invoicing](https://www.bouwflow.be/en/features/invoicing)
- Capterra feature list: job costing, budget tracking, time and expense tracking, invoicing, digital signature, document management — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10026884/Bouwflow/)
- Bills of quantities, pre/post-calculation, work orders via "OnSitePro" app, purchasing, inventory (per a competitor's shortlist) — [Enfin](https://enfin.be/en/blog/best-construction-software-belgium)
- Vorderingsstaten only from the Pro plan, meetstaten in the Business plan — [Taklo](https://taklo.nl/beste/software-voor-belgische-installatiebedrijven)
- Price from EUR 37 per user per month, billed monthly/quarterly/yearly, plus "opstartkost op maat"; only the entry price is public, per-plan prices on request — [UNIVO blog](https://univo.be/blog/beste-erp-software-bouw), [Taklo](https://taklo.nl/beste/software-voor-belgische-installatiebedrijven)
- Companyweb integration — [Companyweb](https://www.companyweb.be/en/integrations/449/bouwflow)
- Embuild Plus partner with sponsored coverage — [Embuild Plus](https://embuildplus.be/nl/nieuws/bouwflow-digitale-eenvoud-voor-complexe-bouwprojecten)
- Heavy SEO content strategy (self-ranked #1 in own "Top 10 ERP" list) — [Bouwflow blog](https://www.bouwflow.be/blog/beste-erp-software-bouw)
- One comparison notes small/mid contractors get a clear starting point "without modular complexity", while sole traders may find the ERP approach too extensive — [UNIVO blog](https://univo.be/blog/beste-erp-software-bouw)

### Inferences
- With ~1-3 people and a 2023 start, Bouwflow's depth and support capacity are probably limited versus its feature claims; bus-factor risk is a selling point against it but applies equally to a new solo entrant.
- Its go-to-market (Embuild Plus partnership, SEO comparison articles, free intake call) is a cheap, replicable playbook.

### Gaps
- No independent reviews with content found; no funding information; founders/investors not confirmed beyond LinkedIn.
- No security/hosting/ISO statement found; Checkinatwork, Exact/Octopus/Yuki links, AI features and client portal not confirmed.
- 1,000+ users claim unverified.

## 4. Visma Bouwsoft (Bouwsoft / Groensoft / Archisoft)

### Takeaway
The long-established Flemish incumbent (Use IT Group, founded 1999; acquired by Visma in July 2021) with 10,000+ construction professionals on subscription and a low headline price (EUR 69-79/month + EUR 865 start-up), sold modularly. Strong on Belgian site administration, nacalculatie and regiewerken; repeatedly criticised for a dated interface, hybrid (not fully cloud) architecture and lagging mobile.

### Cited Findings
**Company facts**
- Visma acquired UseItGroup (Bouwsoft, Groensoft, Archisoft) — announced July 2021; brands continue independently, management stays, price undisclosed — [Dutch IT Channel](https://dutchitchannel.nl/677424/visma-koopt-belgische-useitgroup-bouwsoft.html), [Visma newsroom](https://www.visma.com/newsroom/visma-gears-up-within-the-construction-industry-by-acquiring-useitgroup-bouwsoft-2ea1c83c)
- Founded 1999 as Use IT Group; supports 10,000+ construction professionals via subscriptions; brands Bouwsoft/BatiLogic (construction, installers), Groensoft/VertLogic (gardeners), Archisoft (architects) — [Visma.be](https://www.visma.be/onze-bedrijven/visma-bouwsoft)
- Belgian entity Visma Bouwsoft NV (0466.415.194), address Zuienkerke; gross margin EUR 3.79M in 2025, revenue growth indicator -5.56% for 2025 (third-party rendering of filings, unverified) — [Pappers.be](https://www.pappers.be/nl/company/visma-bouwsoft-0466415194)
- Staff: 11-50 (Joobs); 10-19 (Pappers) — [Joobs](https://joobs.be/en/company/u1R5qmRgnu9tieGt8GMA), [Pappers.be](https://www.pappers.be/nl/company/visma-bouwsoft-0466415194)
- Parent Visma: EUR 3.1bn revenue and 2.4M customers in 2025 — [Accountancy Vanmorgen](https://www.accountancyvanmorgen.nl/2026/03/19/omzet-visma-boven-3-miljard-klantenbestand-explodeert/)

**Features**
- Base package: cost-price calculation, quantity calculation, financial follow-up; optional modules include nacalculatie, vorderingsstaten, management analysis, stock, tenders/price requests, time registration; "floating license" — [Bouwsoft price page](https://www.bouwsoft.be/nl/prijs-visma-bouwsoft/prijs-voor-bouwprofessionals)
- One-click first vorderingsstaat and slips (grond- en wegeniswerken page) — [Bouwsoft](https://www.bouwsoft.be/nl/voor-wie/voor-bouwprofessionals/grond-en-wegeniswerken)

- Integrations: Billit (Peppol sending), Yuki sync of invoices and vorderingsstaten via third-party connectors, Companyweb — [Billit](https://www.billit.eu/nl-be/functies/integraties/visma-bouwsoft-integratie/), [Datapanda](https://datapanda.eu/en/connectors/visma-bouwsoft), [Companyweb](https://www.companyweb.be/en/integrations/34/visma-bouwsoft)
- Described as strong in document management, attestations and site administration; well established in Belgium — [UNIVO blog](https://univo.be/blog/beste-erp-software-bouw)

**Pricing (sources conflict)**
- Vendor page: EUR 69/month plus one-off start-up cost EUR 865, same for builders, installers, garden contractors, architects — [Bouwsoft price page](https://www.bouwsoft.be/nl/prijs-visma-bouwsoft/prijs-voor-bouwprofessionals)

**Security**
- Visma group ISMS is ISO 27001 certified with independently audited Visma Cloud Delivery Model; a 2025 Nemko certificate lists Visma Bouwsoft NV, BE among entities — [Visma Trust Centre](https://www.visma.com/trust-centre/vismaclouddelivery), [Nemko certificate PDF](https://www.tripletex.no/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Visma-iso-27001-2025.pdf)

**Sentiment / weaknesses**
- Dated interface, heavy to use, expensive and slow implementation, cloud and mobile lag behind newer platforms (vendor-authored blog) — [UNIVO blog](https://univo.be/blog/beste-erp-software-bouw)
- Robaws (competitor) says Bouwsoft runs a hybrid, not fully cloud, architecture with a dated interface — [Robaws vs Bouwsoft](https://robaws.com/nl-BE/vergelijking-robaws-vs-bouwsoft-visma)
- Older review round-up: users find too little data to derive actions from figures; updates do not always go smoothly — [Vertuoza blog (competitor)](https://www.vertuoza.com/nl-be/blog/top-12-beste-bouwsoftware-van-2024)
- Support help center on Zendesk — [Bouwsoft support](https://vismabouwsoft-useitgroup.zendesk.com/hc/nl)

### Inferences
- The low headline price is for a base licence; a contractor needing nacalculatie + vorderingsstaten + stock + time registration pays for extra modules, so effective price is materially higher (exact module prices not retrieved).
- Visma ownership gives Bouwsoft the strongest verifiable security credential (entity named on an ISO 27001 certificate), a point a solo developer cannot match.
- The "legacy/hybrid/dated UI" weakness is asserted mainly by competitors; it is consistent across them but lacks independent review evidence in what was retrieved.

### Gaps
- No Trustpilot/Google/app-store ratings retrieved; no evidence found on price increases after the Visma takeover.
- Module-by-module prices, contract terms, Checkinatwork link, API openness not confirmed.
- Revenue figure not available (only gross margin via Pappers, unverified).

## 5. Other players active in Belgium (brief)

### Takeaway
The market is crowded at the generic "offerte-to-factuur" layer with many low-priced Belgian SaaS tools (EUR 30-70 per user/month), and a new wave of small entrants (UNIVO, Enfin, Taklo, Trustup Pro, Buildbase, Prezapp) all fighting with SEO comparison articles. Coverage of several names in the brief could not be verified in this session.

## 6. Gaps for concrete / ruwbouw contractors

### Takeaway
No source found shows any of the Belgian all-in-one packages offering concrete-specific functionality (m3-based nacalculatie, concrete delivery-note matching, formwork m2, rebar kg, equipment/materieel tracking); all are trade-agnostic with generic article/hour-based costing. This is an absence of evidence from search, not proof of absence.

### Cited Findings
- A targeted search for concrete/ruwbouw ERP in Belgium returned only generic packages (Robaws, AFAS, Bouwsoft, C-PRO, Arnsoft, Odoo); none advertise m3 concrete, formwork or rebar handling — [Robaws bouwsoftware](https://robaws.com/nl-BE/bouwsoftware), [Bouwsoft voor bouwprofessionals](https://www.bouwsoft.be/nl/voor-wie/voor-bouwprofessionals), [Buildbase](https://www.buildbase.be/erp-bouw)
- Bouwsoft's nacalculatie is framed around hours and materials entered from the site and regiewerken — [Digital Construction World](https://www.digitalconstructionworld.be/visma-bouwsoft-duidelijk-zicht-op-winstgevendheid-door-nacalculatie/)
- Bouwsoft segments by trade pages (aannemers, grond- en wegeniswerken, installateurs, tuinaannemers, architecten); no dedicated concrete/ruwbouw page surfaced — [Bouwsoft voor wie](https://www.bouwsoft.be/nl/voor-wie)
- Robaws is called a pure construction niche player whose scalability is limited for complex operations — [SpotSaaS](https://www.spotsaas.com/product/robaws)
- Vertuoza's planning is basic and its integrations limited, with no documented open API — [Le Conduc](https://leconduc.fr/logiciel-batiment/logiciel-gestion-chantier/avis-vertuoza/)

### Inferences
- A concrete-contractor vertical (per-pour m3 budget vs delivered vs invoiced, delivery-note/supplier-invoice matching, formwork and rebar quantities per element, pump/crane/formwork rental tracking) looks unserved by the incumbents' standard products; incumbents could approximate it with custom fields and articles (Robaws) but not natively.
- The realistic positioning for a solo developer is a vertical add-on or a bespoke tool for one client, ideally integrating with (not replacing) invoicing/Peppol/accounting which incumbents and tools like Billit already commoditise.
- Incumbents' weaknesses to exploit: Vertuoza (lock-in, support, opaque price), Bouwsoft (dated UX, modular cost creep), Robaws (depth only in the top tier, generic), Bouwflow (tiny team, unproven).

### Gaps
- No vendor was asked directly; no demo or documentation review was possible, so native support for m3/formwork/rebar cannot be excluded.
- No information found on niche concrete tools used in Belgium (e.g. batching-plant delivery portals, rebar scheduling software) or on what mid-size ruwbouw firms actually use (often Excel or in-house tools — unverified).
- No market-size figures for concrete/ruwbouw contractors in Belgium were collected in this scope.
